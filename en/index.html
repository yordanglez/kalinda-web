Common questions

How accurate is the photo estimate?

It's an estimate, not a scale. It identifies the food well and gets close on the portion, but it can drift — especially with sauces, oils and mixed dishes. That's why you can always adjust the amounts before saving. For tracking and spotting trends it's more than enough; for a strict medical diet, talk to a professional.

What happens to my photos?

The photo is sent to be analysed for the estimate, and isn't kept on our servers beyond that moment. It is never used for advertising or shared with anyone else. The detail is in the privacy policy.

Do I need an internet connection?

Yes — to sync your data with your account and for photo recognition. That's what lets you switch phones without losing anything.

Can I delete my account?

Yes, from Settings inside the app, and your whole history goes with it. If you'd rather ask in writing, see how to delete your data.

Does Kalinda replace a dietitian?

No. It's a tool for keeping track of what you eat and seeing how you're doing. It doesn't give medical advice and isn't meant to treat any condition.
